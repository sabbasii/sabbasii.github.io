Project 01

Multimodal analysis of white matter disease and cognitive decline

An ONDRI-based study connecting genomic variation, quantitative MRI, longitudinal cognition, and vascular risk to investigate immune–vascular mechanisms of white matter disease.

  • Integrates harmonized 3T MRI, genotyping, cognitive, demographic, clinical, and vascular-risk data from approximately 520 participants.
  • Combines variant-dosage and gene-burden features with pathway analysis, regularized modeling, and interpretable multimodal integration.
  • Includes a baseline proof-of-concept model for 129 participants with a mean five-fold cross-validated ROC-AUC of approximately 0.72; this is not a validated clinical model.
Multi-Modal Machine Learning to Uncover Genetic and Immune Pathways Driving WMH and Cognitive Decline

Project 02

Evidence-grounded knowledge graph for stroke multi-omics

A literature-derived knowledge graph and retrieval-augmented generation framework for contextualizing molecular findings and supporting traceable hypothesis generation within the Canadian Precision Health Initiative.

  • Defines a stroke-focused vocabulary and evidence model spanning genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics, pathways, treatments, and outcomes.
  • Adapts the RADOR architecture for document ingestion, biomedical entity and relation extraction, normalization, graph construction, and knowledge-graph–augmented retrieval.
  • Retains provenance, directionality, assertion status, biological context, assay information, and reported quantitative evidence for systematic source-level evaluation.
Genome Canada / Canadian Precision Health Initiative · approximately 3,200 patients with stroke

Project 03

Reproducible miRNA–mRNA analysis in a transient mouse MCAO model

A public, version-controlled workflow for connecting paired miRNA and mRNA microarray measurements to treatment-associated molecular responses after stroke.

  • Documents sample matching, quality control, normalization, annotation, and differential-expression analysis for paired molecular layers.
  • Uses late multi-omics integration to connect treatment-associated miRNAs and genes through documented or predicted target relationships.
  • Provides transparent paired-association and pathway-analysis steps through a reproducible R/Bioconductor workflow.
