Sima Abbasi

Postdoctoral Scholar · Computational & Translational Neuroscientist

I am a computational and translational neuroscientist at the University of Alberta, working across interdisciplinary programs with ABNeuro and the Canadian Precision Health Initiative. I integrate genomics, quantitative neuroimaging, longitudinal clinical and cognitive data, and multi-omics to develop interpretable, reproducible analytical frameworks for neurological disease research. My work connects complex biomedical data to biological mechanisms and clinically relevant questions across stroke, cerebral small-vessel disease, neurodegeneration, and cognitive decline.
