Professional Summary

Computational biologist and neuroscientist with expertise in analyzing genomic, transcriptomic, and imaging data using Python, R, and machine learning. Applied machine learning algorithms to extract insights from diverse datasets in preclinical stroke research. Skilled in building reproducible pipelines, integrating multi-omics data, and delivering actionable findings in collaborative settings.

Experience

Postdoctoral Fellow – University of Alberta, Edmonton, AB (Mar 2025 – Present)
  • Clinical Research Lab: Analyzed genetic profiles of stroke patients to explore immune-based diagnostic markers and correlations with MRI lesion metrics.
  • Computational Lab: Applied machine learning to identify genomic signatures associated with white matter damage and cognitive decline; integrated transcriptomic, MRI, and clinical datasets using Python and R.
PhD Researcher – University of Alberta (2019 – 2024)
  • Led a preclinical stroke imaging project combining vascular physiology, flow analysis, and transcriptomics to uncover microcirculatory failure mechanisms.
  • Applied computational pipelines to quantify vascular flow, vessel structure, and cytokine signaling; used regression and clustering techniques for group comparisons.
  • Optimized neutrophil depletion protocol that enhanced cerebral blood flow and revealed the role of neutrophils in vascular obstruction and neuroinflammation.
  • Collaborated with NeuroTrauma Sciences, LLC to assess the therapeutic effects of Ibudilast in stroke models using vascular and transcriptomic endpoints.

Selected Projects

  • Immune-EV Biomarkers in Stroke: Analyzed immune cell–derived extracellular vesicles in stroke patients to evaluate biomarker–outcome associations using Python-based statistical analysis.
  • Multimodal Vascular Profiling: Designed data pipelines to merge in-vivo imaging with post-mortem tissue features to understand microvascular failure and treatment effects.
  • Transcriptomics Pipeline: Built reproducible RNASeq workflows for DEG, enrichment (GO/KEGG), and gene set clustering using edgeR, clusterProfiler, and STRING/Cytoscape.
